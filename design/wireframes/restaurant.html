FIFO queue - data structures in use
Orders are served in the order they were placed. The queue is a custom linked-list implementation (enqueue O(1), dequeue O(1), peek O(1)). It is rebuilt from the database on every restart, so a crash cannot desynchronise it.

The order is removed from the queue, the customer is refunded if paid, stock is restored, and the customer is notified with the reason (Out of stock / Too busy / Closing soon).

  • 3 Two managers accepting the same order at once: only one succeeds (optimistic lock on the order version).
  • 4 Auto-reject after 15 minutes of no action is a SHOULD-tier feature, not shown here.
